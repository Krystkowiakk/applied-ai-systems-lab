---
description: What the morning briefing is, who reads it and when.
---
The shift lead reads the briefing at 06:00, before the production meeting. It answers three
questions about the night: where the line stoppage started, which corrective work orders cannot
start for lack of a part, and which of tonight's batches the quality gate releases (the others
are held). Each answer comes from
the agent responsible for that system; the briefing does not investigate anything itself.
